# Code of conduct

TestHarbor contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate of different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Give technical feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept correction, acknowledge mistakes, and help others participate.
- Protect private information and never pressure someone to disclose identity, credentials, employer information, or confidential code.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Abusive use of issue reports, reviews, or security disclosures.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using the maintainer contact method listed on the project profile. Reports will be handled confidentially to the extent possible. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior is incompatible with this policy.

This policy applies to official project spaces and to public conduct when someone is representing the project.
